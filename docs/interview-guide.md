# InvoiceCraft interview guide

Use this for five short conversations with freelancers, contractors, or small
agency owners who have dealt with crypto payments. Ten minutes is enough. Do
not fill in answers after the fact or turn guesses into quotes.

## Opening

"I'm testing a demo that lets a client pay an invoice with XRP while the
contractor receives an exact test stablecoin amount on Coston2. This is a
testnet prototype, not a live payment product. I want your honest reaction,
especially the parts you would not trust."

Ask permission to keep anonymous notes. Do not record a name, wallet address,
email address, or company name.

## Questions

1. How do clients usually pay you now? What part of that process causes the
   most trouble?
2. Have you accepted XRP or another crypto asset for work? Tell me about the
   last time, including anything that went wrong.
3. If your invoice says $500 but the client wants to pay in XRP, what would you
   need to see before accepting the payment?
4. Look at the InvoiceCraft pay page and receipt. What is clear? What makes you
   hesitate?
5. The demo pays an exact stablecoin amount after the XRP payment is final.
   Would that be useful to you, or would you rather receive XRP directly? Why?
6. If the second chain step were delayed, what would you expect the product to
   show or let you do?
7. What is the first thing that would stop you from using this with a real
   client?

Use follow-up questions when an answer is vague: "Can you give me an example?"
or "What happened next?" Avoid explaining or defending the product during the
interview.

## Notes template

Copy this block once per participant:

```text
Participant ID: P1
Role:
Interviewed on: YYYY-MM-DD
Main finding:
Truthful: yes
Anonymized: yes
```

The main finding should capture something the person actually said or did. A
useful note is specific enough to affect the product, for example which proof
they looked for, which label confused them, or what recovery action they
expected. It should not be a generic compliment.

## Optional research record

Interviews are optional and do not block hackathon acceptance. If you conduct
them later, give each record a unique participant ID, a role, an ISO date, and
a finding of at least one full sentence. Only set `truthful` and `anonymized`
to `true` after checking the note.

For demo rehearsal without participants, use the clearly labelled
[simulated interview scenarios](simulated-interviews.md). They are fictional
and must not be submitted as research evidence.
